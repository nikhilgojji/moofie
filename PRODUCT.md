# Product <!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

College students who use Canvas or CatCourses on phones and laptops and want one faster place to check coursework, grades, deadlines, announcements, modules, files, and submissions.

## Product Purpose

Moofie turns Canvas course data into a simpler daily workspace. Success means a student can understand what is due, open course material, review grades, and submit supported assignments without returning to Canvas for routine work.

## Positioning

Moofie is a student-focused layer over Canvas: it keeps the institutional course data while reorganizing it around the small set of tasks students repeat every day.

## Operating Context

Students move between a weekly dashboard, individual courses, assignments, files and PDFs, grade details, and notifications. The interface must work comfortably on desktop and mobile, including as an installed PWA.

## Capabilities & Constraints

- Canvas remains the source of truth for course and submission data.
- Moofie supports native browsing of course modules, announcements, assignments, pages, quizzes, and files where the Canvas API exposes them.
- Supported file-upload assignments can be submitted from Moofie.
- External tools may still require a new browser window.
- Light and dark themes must remain equally usable.
- Existing account, grade-calculation, notification, PWA, and course-navigation behavior must remain intact during visual changes.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Moofie.
- The app icon is a centered “M” inside a black circle.
- Interface language is short, direct, and student-friendly.
- The product should feel simple and calm rather than decorative, loud, or institutional.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real Canvas/CatCourses data is rendered throughout the application.
- User-provided screenshots compare Moofie against Canvas and establish the desired information hierarchy.
- User-provided workspace-interface references establish the new quality bar for color, typography, density, and organization.

## Product Principles

- Put daily student tasks before LMS complexity.
- Show each fact once, close to the action or object it describes.
- Preserve access to real course content without visual clutter.
- Keep desktop and mobile experiences equally complete.
- Prefer calm hierarchy and precise spacing over heavy weight or excessive borders.
